# The Three Daily Wins — revised design brief

## The idea

**A book, not an application.** A luxury editorial object comes to life directly on the webpage. The visitor watches a charcoal hardcover open and warm ivory pages turn themselves. The presentation asks for no input and makes no assessment of the reader.

The Three Daily Wins is the **only personal experience** beneath Sony Alexis’s existing biography. The corporate homepage, four ventures, original biography and product/customer systems remain unchanged. Daily Devotion and Blog are removed from this revised build’s personal navigation, route manifest and sitemap.

## The five-part sequence

1. **Cover — THE THREE DAILY WINS.** Champagne-gold foil/emboss treatment, fine double rules, a small III seal and restrained cloth-like texture on charcoal hardcover.
2. **Chapter I — SPIRITUAL.** Warm paper, a large Roman I and quiet editorial language about faith, presence and purpose.
3. **Chapter II — PHYSICAL.** Roman II, expressive serif typography and language about movement, care and vitality. No tasks, measurements or health promises.
4. **Chapter III — INTELLECTUAL.** Roman III, curiosity, learning and perspective. No quiz, challenge or completion requirement.
5. **Final page — THREE WINS. ONE DAY. A BETTER YOU.** A typographic closing spread. The final page remains still; the book does not loop indefinitely.

Supporting chapter lines are new editorial presentation copy, not quotations, personal testimony or a claim of an existing published physical book.

## Materials and direction

- Charcoal hardcover; subdued cloth grain, recessed hinge, fine gold framing and champagne lettering.
- Warm ivory stock, fine paper grain, individual edge layers, curved corners and a defined gutter.
- Large serif chapter numerals and editorial titles; small, spaced running heads and understated folios.
- Soft directional lighting, contact shadows and three-dimensional depth; no particles, confetti, neon, bouncing or audio.
- One coordinated visual language, with the tactile object—not interface chrome—as the focal point.

## Choreography

A finite **30-second** presentation begins automatically once the book is visible. The cover holds for about three seconds, opens over 2.8 seconds, then each chapter is given a calm reading interval before a 2.8-second turn. Four articulated segments bend each moving leaf; uninterrupted faces restore clean ink and continuous paper grain at rest. The camera gently settles from a closed-cover view into a complete spread.

The presentation pauses when the tab or book is not visible and resumes without skipping ahead. It is driven by an animation frame timeline, not a video, slideshow crossfade, click gesture or carousel library.

## Desktop and mobile

Desktop presents the complete open spread in a spacious, softly lit setting. Mobile uses a closer right-page composition, with the left cover/spread deliberately extending beyond the scene edge so the text does not shrink into a tiny two-page thumbnail. The scene clips that cinematic crop without horizontal document scrolling. Short landscape layouts adapt their book size and stage height.

## Absolutely no application functionality

No writing fields, checkboxes, completion buttons, task tracking, progress ring, dashboard, forms, quizzes, swipe/click navigation, next/previous arrows, replay button, timer display, account or saved state. No requests to a Bible API, CMS, tracker or other third party. No browser storage is read or written.

Standard browser scrolling, reload and operating-system accessibility preferences are not replaced with custom controls.

## Accessible alternative

Reduced-motion users and browsers without JavaScript receive a still, typeset reading edition containing the complete sequence. Screen readers receive the same chapters in reading order, without duplicated panel text or timed announcements. A live switch to reduced motion also stops the moving scene. The default presentation intentionally has no on-page animation controls, as requested; automated checks alone must not be described as full WCAG certification.

## Review boundary

Build the actual webpage and review its real desktop/mobile browser captures and working preview **before any production deployment**. This brief supersedes the earlier interactive Three Daily Wins dashboard. It does not authorize changing the corporate homepage, publishing private CMS material, provisioning services or deploying this revision without design review.
