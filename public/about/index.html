THE IDEA IS SIMPLE

Build strength.
Give progress a name.

Monte Wo turns the work you put in into a climb you can follow. Not someone else’s finish line. Your next step.

Rooted in the climb

“Monte Wo” means “climb high” in Haitian Creole. Our signature—Leve. Klase. Monte Wo.—brings together lifting, ranking, and the determination to keep rising.

Our nine-rank identity is Debitan → Bati → Solid → Fò → Avanse → Mèt → Chanpyon → Titan → Lejand. Each name marks a step, not your worth as a person.

What you can use today

  • Six free tools: 1RM, plates, protein, TDEE, macros, and body fat. Inputs are not uploaded or saved by these tools.
  • A separate device-local educational strength estimate, with no rank awarded from unapproved standards.
  • MONTE WO Strength Index™: four independent disciplines, draft rulebooks and nine earned rank identities.
  • An optional launch waitlist when the signup service is configured.
  • Offline access to cached pages and the calculator after an online visit.

What we are building

Workout logging, per-set scores/ranks, progress tracking, streaks, downloadable animated guides, app accounts, and Friends/Global/Regional leaderboards are in development. Explore the coming-soon core app. The phone screens on the homepage are illustrative concepts, not evidence of released features or real user results.

Who operates Monte Wo

VitaFlex Business Group manages MONTE WO, operating as an individual or sole proprietor. The legal jurisdiction has not yet been confirmed. We will update the legal pages as those details are finalized.
