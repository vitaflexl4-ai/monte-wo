No public app account registration is available.

A waitlist signup is not an app account. A provisioned ranking-pilot participant may have separately stored performance and private evidence records; request their review or deletion through the operator. No automatic server deletion is claimed. Choose the right action below: remove a signup, ask about account data, or clear information stored only in your browser.

1. Remove your waitlist signup

Send an email from the address you used to sign up, with the subject MONTE WO — Waitlist deletion request. Ask us to remove your email and associated signup details. This also withdraws consent for future waitlist launch updates.

The operator will verify the request and remove the matching active waitlist record. The address, chosen language, signup timestamp, consent record, and website source label are included in that removal unless retention is legally required. Limited copies may remain temporarily in provider backups.

2. Request account or other personal-data deletion

If you have used another Monte Wo release with an account, or have sent personal information to support, contact us from the associated email address. Specify which account or information you want deleted. Do not send a password or identity document in your initial request.

We may need to verify control of the address and clarify the scope before deletion. We will explain any information that must be retained for legal or security reasons. Applicable response deadlines depend on the relevant law; a fixed processing deadline has not yet been confirmed.

CONTACT THE OPERATOR

Prepare a deletion request.

This opens a draft in your email app. It does not send a message or delete server data automatically.

Or email Montewo@vitaflexbusinessgroup.com directly. Check your email app and press Send to submit your request.

3. Clear data on this device

This removes this website’s saved calculator inputs and language preference, deletes Monte Wo’s cached site/media files, and unregisters this site’s offline service worker in this browser. It does not remove a waitlist entry or delete an app account on a server.
