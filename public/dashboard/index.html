    VERIFICATION / NOT CONNECTEDPLACEHOLDER · NOT IMPLEMENTED IN STEP 1

    Provisional and MONTE Verified

    Self-reported performances are Provisional. MONTE Verified requires evidence reviewed and approved by an authorized reviewer. Verification never raises a rank by itself, athletes cannot approve their own evidence, and reviewer decisions are kept as an audit history.

    PLANNED · NOT IN STEP 1

    Planned for the MVP

    • Evidence upload per performance
    • Independent reviewer decisions with reasons
    • Immutable review and audit history
