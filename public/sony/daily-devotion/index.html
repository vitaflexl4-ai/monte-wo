BE STILL

01 / SCRIPTURE PRACTICE

An excerpt for Scripture memorization, not a published devotion.

Psalm 46:10 · EXCERPT

“Be still, and know that I am God”

Pause. Recall the words.
Let them come back slowly.

King James Version (public domain)

Read, pause, then recall.

02 / PERSONAL RESPONSE

Read. Reflect.
Respond.

Optional prompts, not a published reflection by Sony Alexis. Your responses stay with you; nothing is submitted or saved.

01Reflection+

Read slowly. What does the passage say? Notice its words before drawing a conclusion.

02REPENT+

Where might you need to turn toward God? Take time for an honest response.

03PUT OFF+

Consider one attitude or habit you need to set aside. Keep your response specific.

04PUT ON+

Consider the faithful alternative. What would putting it into practice look like?

05Application+

Choose one small, concrete action you can take today.

06Prayer+

Bring your response to God in your own words. There is no required script here.
