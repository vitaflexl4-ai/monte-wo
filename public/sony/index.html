Roots & gratitude

My journey has been shaped by family, faith, education, and a deep appreciation for the opportunities and experiences that life has afforded me. Above all, it has been guided by a love for Haiti and a belief in the importance of contributing, in whatever capacity one can, to something greater than oneself.

I have been fortunate to grow with the support of a family whose sacrifices, encouragement, and presence have made much of my journey possible. I remain deeply grateful for their confidence in me and hope, through my choices and actions, to honor what they have invested in my life.

Learning & building

My interest in leadership began early. From primary school onward, I had the privilege of serving as head of my class, experiences that introduced me to responsibility, communication, and the importance of understanding those around me. I later earned a bachelor's degree in Business Administration and a Master of Business Administration (MBA), alongside professional certifications in personal training, artificial intelligence, and Bloomberg trading.

My interests extend across business, fitness, technology, and innovation. Fitness, in particular, holds a special place in my life. Beyond the physical benefits, I appreciate the discipline, patience, consistency, and self-awareness it encourages. I believe that caring for one's physical well-being is also an investment in one's overall quality of life. It has reinforced my appreciation for personal development and the value of steady progress.

Through VitaFlex Business Group, I explore the development of independent ventures and opportunities that can create lasting value. I have also spent approximately eight years developing the vision behind VitaFlex Intelligence Corp, a project I intend to introduce when it is ready.

Perspective & responsibility

Over time, my understanding of leadership and human nature has continued to evolve. I have learned that listening can be as valuable as speaking, that understanding often requires patience, and that not every situation calls for a response. People, circumstances, and perspectives are rarely as simple as they first appear. I believe good judgment comes from remaining observant, open-minded, and willing to reconsider one's assumptions.

One of life's more humbling lessons has been recognizing that the world does not revolve around any one of us. In my younger years, I sometimes approached life with the confidence of someone who believed the world needed him to keep turning. Experience gradually offered a different perspective. We are each part of something much larger, and perhaps our greatest opportunity is to understand our place within it and make our presence worthwhile.

I have come to appreciate that leadership is not measured solely by position, visibility, or influence, but by judgment, character, consistency, and the manner in which we treat others. I value discretion over unnecessary attention, substance over appearances, and thoughtful action over the desire to be seen.

Faith, Haiti & service

My faith in God remains central to my life, providing guidance, perspective, and a reminder of the importance of humility. My love for Haiti is equally close to my heart. I remain hopeful about its future and believe that meaningful progress begins with responsibility, dignity, and a willingness to contribute.

I am grateful to pursue life as one who serves. I remain ambitious, but I have come to understand that ambition is most meaningful when accompanied by patience, discipline, gratitude, and consideration for others. There is still much to learn, much to understand, and much to give.

I hope to live with enough wisdom to keep learning, enough humility to keep listening, and enough conviction to keep serving.

The journey continues.

Sony Alexis
