BEFORE YOU CLIMB

A few good questions.

A clear picture of where we are, and where we’re going.

What is Monte Wo?

MONTE WO is a strength-focused product by VitaFlex Business Group. The public website offers free calculators and written exercise guides. The future app and nine-rank system are being developed; official ranking standards are not approved.

Are the ranks official strength standards?

Not yet. All launch standards are DRAFT — NOT OFFICIAL. No official rank is awarded until its discipline methodology and thresholds are independently approved.

What works without internet?

After a successful first online visit and service-worker installation, this website and its calculator can reopen offline in a supported browser. Workout logging, account sync, and downloadable animations are planned, not available in this build. Browser storage may be cleared or evicted.

Can I download the app yet?

The native app is in development. There are no App Store or Google Play releases yet. You can use the calculator here now, or add this website to your home screen where your browser supports it.

What does “Leve. Klase. Monte Wo.” mean?

Lift. Rank. Climb high. Rooted in Haitian Creole. The homepage can be read in English, Kreyòl Ayisyen and French. Other website sections are currently English-first; wider translation is planned.
