Overhead press.

Equipment symbol only—not exercise technique.
MOVE WITH INTENTION

How to approach it

  1. Set a light bar at upper-chest height in a rack. Grip just outside shoulder width.
  2. Brace gently and press overhead while moving your head only enough to clear the bar.
  3. Lower to the starting position under control.
KEEP IN MIND

Avoid leaning back to finish the press.
