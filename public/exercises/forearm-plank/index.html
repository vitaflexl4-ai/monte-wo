Forearm plank.

Abdominals WRITTEN STARTER GUIDE

NO MATCHING REPOSITORY VIDEO

This exercise has a written guide, but no exact-ID video in the supplied packs.

No different exercise has been substituted.
MOVE WITH INTENTION

How to approach it

  1. Place forearms on the floor with elbows under shoulders.
  2. Extend the legs or keep knees down and gently brace the trunk.
  3. Hold only while you can breathe and keep a controlled position, then rest.
KEEP IN MIND

Keep hips in line with the trunk rather than chasing a longer hold.
