MOVE WITH INTENTION

How to approach it

  1. Attach a rope or bar to a high cable and select a light load.
  2. Keep upper arms near your sides and extend the elbows to press down.
  3. Let the elbows bend again slowly without letting the stack slam.
KEEP IN MIND

Move at the elbow, not by leaning your whole body onto the attachment.
