Push-up.

Pectorals WRITTEN STARTER GUIDE

Open original repository video ↗
MOVE WITH INTENTION

How to approach it

  1. Place hands on the floor or a stable elevated surface, slightly wider than shoulders.
  2. Keep your trunk supported as you bend the elbows and lower with control.
  3. Push the surface away and return without letting the hips sag.
KEEP IN MIND

Move shoulders and hips together; keep breathing.

START WHERE YOU ARE

Make it more manageable

Use a wall or higher surface to reduce the load.

Train with care

Choose a stable surface and stop if you feel wrist or shoulder pain.
