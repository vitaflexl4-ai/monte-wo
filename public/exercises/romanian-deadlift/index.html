Romanian deadlift.

Hamstrings · Glutes WRITTEN STARTER GUIDE

SOURCE LOOP · 4.3 s

Open original repository video ↗
MOVE WITH INTENTION

How to approach it

  1. Stand with light dumbbells in front of your thighs and knees slightly bent.
  2. Push the hips back while keeping the weights close and your trunk steady.
  3. Stop before losing control of your back position; drive the hips forward to stand.
KEEP IN MIND

Think hips back, not weights to the floor.
