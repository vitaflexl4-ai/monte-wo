# MONTE WO — Owner Decisions and Approvals

Updated: 2026-10-23

## Approved direction
- Owner instructed the team to reinvent MONTE WO's ranking standards and app experience on 2026-10-23.
- Launch disciplines: Barbell Squat, Barbell Bench Press, Barbell Deadlift, Strict Biceps Curl.
- Independent nine-rank ladder: Debitan, Bati, Solid, Fô, Avanse, Mèt, Chanpyon, Titan, Lejand.
- Product principles and release gates are documented in `docs/MONTE-WO-REINVENTION-STANDARD-v0.1.md`.

## Still requires explicit owner decision
- **Final numerical cutoffs:** a new candidate table must be benchmarked and reviewed before it becomes official. Do not invent values or ship arbitrary cutoffs.
- **Body-mass/category policy:** approve which categories are supported and the formula/model before applying rank cutoffs.
- **Verification operations:** approve reviewer eligibility, acceptable video evidence, privacy/retention, rejection and appeal process before verified ranks are issued.
- **Authentication method:** choose sign-in method before production auth is built.
- **Haitian Creole and French:** native-speaker approval required; do not machine-translate or populate unapproved `name_ht` values.
- **Legal and privacy pages:** approve legal language and store data-safety answers.
- **Release identity and accounts:** confirm canonical Android application ID and create/approve Apple/Google developer accounts before any release process.
- **Public download origin and artifact:** confirm permanent origin and approve a signed, verified release artifact before any download link is presented as live.

## Guardrails
No production deployment, remote migration, real-account creation, store publishing/build, or spending without separate explicit approval.
